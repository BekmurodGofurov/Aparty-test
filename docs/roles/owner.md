# Role: Owner

The **Owner** role is designed for individual property owners participating in the Aparty marketplace. Owners provide the core inventory of the platform by listing their available apartments and rooms.

## Capabilities and Permissions
- **Listing Creation & Management**: Owners can create, edit, and manage listings for their properties. They define property attributes, upload images, and set availability.
- **Availability Management**: Control over the occupancy state of their listings. Owners dictate how many spaces are available and the target demographic (e.g., target gender).
- **Booking Workflow**: 
  - Owners receive incoming rental requests from tenants (Guests/Students).
  - They have the authority to accept or reject these requests through their dedicated dashboard.
- **Active Rentals**: Once a request is accepted, owners manage the active rental lifecycle, viewing current tenant details and managing occupancy limits.

## Business Domain Context
The Owner's capabilities are logically centered around the "Listings" and "Rentals" domains. They have authoritative write access over their specific property entities and are the primary decision-makers in the booking transaction lifecycle, regardless of how these transactions are processed architecturally.
